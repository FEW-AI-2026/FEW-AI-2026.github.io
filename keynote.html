Data Science and Wind Energy

Yu Ding

H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering,
Georgia Institute of Technology

Personal Website Watch on YouTube
Abstract

The speaker published a book in 2019, entitled 'Data Science for Wind Energy,' in which a set of wind operational problems is framed through a general formulation of f(Y|X), where Y is the response on a turbine and X is the wind and environmental input. When Y is the wind power output, the conditional density, f(Y|X), is related to the power curve model of wind turbines and can be used to characterize a wind turbine's performance and production efficiency. When Y is the mechanical load, the conditional density, f(Y|X), is related to the load estimation and reliability management for the wind turbines. Modeling and understanding the dynamic in X itself is also important, because a good model of X leads to better wind forecasting. The book is therefore structured in three parts: wind field modeling for forecasting, power curve modeling for performance analysis, and turbine reliability and fault detection. In this talk, the speaker will discuss work related to the second part of the book and present examples in which data science solutions make sensible impact.

AI, Metaverse and Education

Dr. Juan Humberto Sossa

CIC-IPN, Mexico

Personal Website Watch on YouTube
Abstract

Artificial Intelligence combines algorithms that allow computers to perceive, learn, solve problems, and make appropriate decisions. A Metaverse, on the other hand, is a shared digital world where users interact through avatars in an immersive 3D virtual space. In this talk, we address the horizontal technology of AI, its foundational role in the establishment of metaverses, future educational applications, and the necessary requirements for human acceptance of the next digital leap.

Brave new world, use of AI in agricultural research

Dr. Kai Sonder

CIMMYT

Personal Website Watch on YouTube
Abstract

Within the CGIAR network, machine learning is applied to extrapolate soil, climate, and pest data with greater accuracy, improving fertilizer recommendations and climate adaptation strategies. Combined with remote sensing, AI enables near real-time insights on crop types, land-use change, and early disease detection. At CIMMYT, AI is also accelerating the analysis of the world's largest maize and wheat gene bank, identifying drought tolerance and breeding resilient varieties, while checking ethical energy and footprint limits.

Navigating the Data Gaps: Time Series Forecasting with Incomplete Information

Dr. Xiaoou Li

Cinvestav, Mexico

Personal Website Watch on YouTube
Abstract

Time series forecasting is a cornerstone of modern data-driven decision-making. However, real-world systems suffer from missing data due to sensory or network failures. This talk will explore building resilient forecasting models that handle these data gaps. Drawing from our work on robust electricity forecasting using a Concept Echo State Network (CESN), we present a novel approach that maintains high accuracy and delivers insights even when faced with imperfect information structures.
