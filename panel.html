Panel Discussions

Bringing together expert researchers to explore crucial paradigms in artificial intelligence.

Panel 1

Education in the AI Era

October 13, 2026 | 15:00 - 16:30 | Host: Dr. Bella Citlali Martinez Seis

Watch Panel on YouTube

1. Ethics, Equity, and Governance of AI in Education: Dra. Lourdes Martínez Villaseñor, a recognized expert and award-winner in Responsible AI Ethics (RAI), will lead a discussion on AI Bias and Equity, Data Privacy, Transparency, and the Educator's Responsibility in framing active institutional safe spaces.

2. Redefining Curriculum, Learning, and Assessment: Dr. Juan Humberto Sossa Azuela and Dr. Xiaoou Li discuss skills such as creativity, critical thinking, and complex problem-solving that must now be prioritized over rote memorization. The shift goes from grading final products to evaluating the comprehensive learning process, a topic Dr. Joselito Medina Marín’s expertise in Optimization of Systems is crucial for.

3. Technical Integration: Databases, Optimization, and Emerging AI Paradigms: Dr. Lisbeth Rodríguez-Mazahua addresses the technical challenges of managing, storing, and utilizing vast amounts of student data effectively for adaptive learning platforms. Dr. Genaro J. Martínez explores technical feasibility via Cellular Automata and Complex Systems to deliver robust intelligent tutoring platforms tailored to individual student speeds.

Panel 2

Opportunities for AI Application in Mexico

October 14, 2026 | 15:00 - 16:30 | Host: Dr. Wen Yu

Watch Panel on YouTube

1. AI in Industrial Automation and Advanced Control: Drawing on vast expertise in control systems, the panel details how Machine Learning is revolutionizing Mexican industry. Dr. Mario César Maya Rodríguez, a specialist in Artificial Neural Networks, will discuss using AI to optimize production and predict equipment failure. Contributions from Dr. Hipolito Aguilar Sierra and Dr. Andrés José Rodríguez Torres highlight mechatronics, intelligent robotics, and precision automation solutions.

2. Social Impact and AI for Public Good: Focusing on quality of life and practical solutions. Dra. Bella Citlali Martinez Seis presents projects on technological developments for health systems and communities with disabilities. Dr. Aguilar Sierra shares insights from the Robotics & AI Solutions iLab on medical rehabilitation systems, while Dr. Luis Arturo Soriano Avendaño explores AI-driven resource modeling in the crucial agricultural sector.

3. Applied Research, Innovation, and Ecosystem Development: Dr. Giner Alor Hernández discusses the role of high-impact scientific publications in fostering technological transfer, ensuring that Mexican AI talent is prepared to lead academic-industry linkages globally based on CONAHCyT framework stays.
