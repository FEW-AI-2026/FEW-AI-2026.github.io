Panel Discussions / Dr. Xiaoou Li

Dr. Xiaoou Li

Cinvestav, Mexico
lixo@cs.cinvestav.mx
Panel 1 Participant

Biography

Xiaoou Li has been a Professor of Computer Science at Cinvestav-IPN (National Polytechnic Institute of Mexico) since 2000. She holds a Ph.D. in Automatic Control and a B.S. in Applied Mathematics from Northeastern University, China, in 1992 and 1995, respectively.

Prior to her current role, she was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) and a lecturer at Northeastern University. She is a Level 3 member of the National Researcher System of Mexico and a member of the Mexican Academy of Science.

Her research interests include machine learning, data mining applications, data-driven sustainable automation, and robot-assisted rehabilitation. She has more than 300 publications. She has held Senior Research Fellow positions at Queen's University Belfast and the University of California Santa Cruz. She has served as General Chair for two major IEEE conferences, ICNSC 2016 and CASE 2022, and Program Chair for SSCI 2023.

She serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Knowledge-Based Systems, IEEE/CAA Journal of Automatica Sinica, and IEEE SMC Magazine. She also served as a member of the IEEE James Mulligan Education Medal Committee.
