Panel Discussions Overview

Opportunities for AI Application in Mexico

Discover the tangible future of Artificial Intelligence in Mexico with a panel of six distinguished researchers who are actively translating cutting-edge AI concepts into real-world applications across industry, health, and agriculture.

1. AI in Industrial Automation and Advanced Control

Drawing on vast expertise in control systems and industrial environments, the panel will detail how AI and Machine Learning are revolutionizing Mexican industry. Dr. Mario César Maya Rodríguez, a specialist in Artificial Neural Networks and Transfer Learning for industrial processes, will discuss using AI to optimize production, predict equipment failure, and implement advanced control systems. Contributions from Dr. Hipolito Aguilar Sierra and Dr. Andrés José Rodríguez Torres, with their extensive backgrounds in Mechatronics, Automatic Control, and Industrial Automation, will highlight the development and implementation of intelligent robotics and automation solutions for greater efficiency and precision.

2. Social Impact and AI for Public Good

The discussion will spotlight AI applications focused on improving quality of life and addressing societal needs in Mexico, moving beyond abstract theory to practical solutions. Dra. Bella Citlali Martinez Seis, a leading researcher in Data Mining and Social Network Analysis, will present projects focused on technological developments for health systems and communities with disabilities, emphasizing human-centered design. Dr. Aguilar Sierra will share insights from his work in Robotics & AI Solutions iLab on medical robotics, including the development of exoskeletons and rehabilitation systems. Additionally, Dr. Luis Arturo Soriano Avendaño, with expertise in Renewable Energy and Agricultural Robotics, will explore how AI-driven modeling and control systems are optimizing resource use in the crucial agricultural sector.

3. Applied Research, Innovation, and Ecosystem Development

This segment will focus on the development pipeline from research publication to technological commercialization. Dr. Giner Alor Hernández, a prolific author and editor in the field of Computer Science and AI, will discuss the role of high-impact scientific publication in fostering technological transfer and strengthening national research capacity. The panelists, with their established records of industry experience, CONAHCyT projects, and international research stays (France, Spain, USA), will discuss strategies for strengthening academic-industry linkages and ensuring that Mexican AI talent is prepared to lead innovation at a global level.

Education in the AI Era

Join six of Mexico's leading researchers in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science for an in-depth and critical exploration of Education in the AI Era. This high-level panel will move beyond generalities to address the technical, ethical, and practical challenges of integrating AI into the academic landscape.

1. Ethics, Equity, and Governance of AI in Education

This segment will delve into the critical ethical and regulatory dimensions of AI integration. Dra. Lourdes Martínez Villaseñor, a recognized expert and award-winner in Responsible AI Ethics (RAI), will lead a discussion on: AI Bias and Equity; Privacy and Transparency; The Educator's Responsibility.

2. Redefining Curriculum, Learning, and Assessment

With the rise of Generative AI, traditional methods of teaching and assessment are fundamentally disrupted. The panel will explore how curricula must evolve to maintain rigor in a world of instant answers. Dr. Juan Humberto Sossa Azuela and Dr. Xiaoou Li, experts in Machine Learning, Pattern Recognition, and Learning in Technology-Rich Environments, will discuss which skills—such as creativity, critical thinking, and complex problem-solving—must now be prioritized over rote memorization. The shift from grading final, potentially AI-generated products (like essays) to evaluating the process of learning, including project-based learning and the application of complex algorithms, a topic Dr. Joselito Medina Marín's expertise in Optimization of Systems and Autómata Theory is crucial for.

3. Technical Integration: Databases, Optimization, and Emerging AI Paradigms

This section will tackle the technical foundation required to support a modern, AI-enabled educational system, drawing on the panelists' deep research backgrounds. Dr. Lisbeth Rodríguez-Mazahua, a specialist in Autonomic Database Systems and Big Data, will address the challenges of managing, storing, and utilizing vast amounts of student data effectively and securely for personalized learning systems. Dr. Genaro J. Martínez, known for his work in Cellular Automata and Complex Systems. Dr. Sossa Azuela and Dr. Li will discuss the technical feasibility and impact of deploying Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) and adaptive learning platforms that tailor content to the individual student's pace and needs using cutting-edge AI.
